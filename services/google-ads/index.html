Questions about Google Ads Management

Is ad spend included in your management fee?

Advertising spend is paid to Google separately. We explain our management scope and fee before you decide.

Can you work on an existing account?

Yes. We start with a review of your existing structure, search terms and tracking, then recommend priorities.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Results depend on demand, competition, budget, your offer and how enquiries are handled. We focus on relevant traffic and clear measurement.
